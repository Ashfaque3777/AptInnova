# AptInnova - AI Solutions for RSMEs

## About
AptInnova empowers small and medium-sized enterprises (RSMEs) with trustworthy, human-centric AI solutions. We build AI tools that are transparent, ethical, and designed to augment human capabilities — not replace them. Founded with a mission to make AI accessible and responsible for businesses of all sizes.

## What We Do
- AI Strategy Consulting — helping organizations define and execute responsible AI roadmaps
- Applied Generative AI — custom LLM-powered solutions for real business workflows
- Ready-to-Use AI Agents — pre-built, deployable AI agents for common enterprise tasks
- Knowledge Assistants — intelligent document retrieval and Q&A systems
- Data Solutions — data pipelines, analytics infrastructure, and AI-ready data preparation
- Business Intelligence — dashboards, reporting, and data-driven decision tools
- Custom Software Development — full-stack web and mobile applications with AI integration
- AI Training & Education — workshops, bootcamps, and corporate training programs

## Products
- InnoVethic — Regulated AI operations platform for European banks. Source-cited regulatory answers (EBA, ECB, DNB, AFM, FATF, BIS, IFRS), governed multi-step workflows, and complete audit trails. Sovereign AI, built in Europe.
- Ainsteins — AI agent platform for business automation. Deploy intelligent agents that handle complex workflows, integrate with existing tools, and operate within governance guardrails.
- A(i)MPACT Lab — AI innovation sandbox for experimenting with and prototyping AI solutions. A safe environment to test ideas, validate concepts, and build proofs of concept.
