## 3. How it's built

One universal core, then depth for whoever wants it. Everyone starts with the same foundation. From there, learners add the modules that fit their role and goals.

**Tier 0 · Foundations**
No prior knowledge needed. Builds real AI literacy and immediate everyday productivity, the skills anyone can use from day one.

**Tier 1 · Applied**
For people who want to put AI to work in their actual job. Practical, role-focused skills across areas like business and marketing, content and design, and operations and productivity.

**Tier 2 · Builder**
The hands-on, technical track for people who want to build with AI. Opt-in, and it includes coding.

Every module stacks onto the last, so learners climb a clear ladder from literacy to building.

---

## 4. How the training runs

**Flexible format.** Run it as a workshop, a weekly course, or a flexible schedule that fits your calendar.

**Real practice.** Short quizzes per module, practical assignments, and a capstone project in each paid tier.

**Certification that lasts.** A certificate for each tier, plus a portfolio project (on GitHub or LinkedIn) that outlasts the certificate and shows real capability.

**Trainers who've done the work.** Industry-experienced trainers, mentored labs, and time to clear doubts, with recorded material for revision.
